Privacy Policy

Pixel Gauntlet: 100 Rounds (package com.trufchev.pixelgauntlet)
Effective date: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Pixel Gauntlet Android game ("the game") handles and why. The game is developed by Trufchev Apps ("we", "us"). You can contact us at trufchevyordan@gmail.com.

1. Your game data stays on your device

  • The game stores your best round, how many times you have beaten all 100 rounds, and any extra lives you have paid for but not used yet. This is kept only in the game's private storage on your device.
  • We do not operate any servers, we do not require an account, and we never receive, read or upload this information.
  • If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the game's data in your device backup to your Google account. This is controlled by your device settings and governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes its data from your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an extra life, and a full-screen ad now and then when you leave a game over screen. They are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address (used to estimate general location),
  • information about the device, the game, and ad interactions,
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Google processes this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Your consent choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy at the top of the title screen. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.

4. Purchases

In the final ten rounds you can buy one extra life per run. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase went through.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our side, there is nothing we hold to access, correct or delete. Your game data is entirely under your control on your device. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can exercise your rights through Google using the links above. If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
